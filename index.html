Guild History

Welcome to the historical home of Loremongers. Founded during the EverQuest craze, moving on to Dark Age of Camelot and eventually World of Warcraft, our band stood strong through countless RvR campaigns, dungeon raids, and realm defense battles.

This archive serves as a permanent memorial to the friends, victories, and memories built across the frontiers. It will not attempt to recreate the guild's original loremongers.com website, as that is mostly someone else's work. You can view that original website at the Wayback Machine. Sadly, my work on Culhwch's Cool Celtic Corner is lost in the ether; I didn't have a copy of it anywhere and the forum it was on is long gone with no web crawls by the Wayback Machine to arcive it.

This page will slowly take shape and is more of a nostalgia project than anything else. But it will bring back good memories.
